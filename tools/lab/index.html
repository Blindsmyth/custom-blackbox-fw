Granular parameters (Gran tab, stock ranges)

Granular is the stock pad engine: the playhead scans at Speed, so it drifts off the tempo. Granular Sync scans at the tempo ratio instead, so the cloud stays on the beat; the grains keep their pitch either way. Stock sums grains without normalising: at high Density it is loud (up to ~2.8×), so watch the meter and Vol.
